# Resume parsing and scoring research

Research checked on 2026-11-11. These public sources inform Resume TeX's diagnostics. They do not disclose enough information to reproduce an employer's parser or hiring rules.

| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## What an extraction test establishes

PDF text extraction establishes which character data this extractor can recover and in what sequence. Geometry can reveal separated columns or suspicious placement. It cannot prove how another vendor groups the same lines into employers, roles, dates, or skills. Reading-order heuristics can flag a legitimate multi-column layout or miss a complex one.

A font can display correctly while encoding unusual ligatures or symbols. Images can contain readable information that a text-only extractor does not recover. Source-level checks for tables and icons are advisory because a macro name does not establish the final PDF structure. A warning must state its evidence and uncertainty.

BM25 is a lexical retrieval method with term-frequency saturation and document-length normalization. Its IDF depends on a declared document collection. A tiny local collection is a heuristic reference set, not an employment-market corpus. Report that choice and parameters rather than implying statistical calibration. [Stanford's BM25 explanation](https://nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book/html/htmledition/okapi-bm25-a-non-binary-model-1.html) describes the underlying model.

Required/preferred classification, synonym expansion, English stemming, section identification, date extraction, and quantified-impact checks all introduce assumptions. Publish those assumptions alongside the implemented formula. Missing evidence should lower confidence rather than become a claim that a candidate lacks a qualification. Agent fixes must ask for factual support instead of inventing achievements or adding unverified skills.

No score here predicts rejection, interview probability, or compliance with all employers' application rules. Review the actual employer form after upload. A successful local extraction is not a test of Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, or Taleo.
